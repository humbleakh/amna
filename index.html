About — Meet Hafiza Amna Waqar
Hafiza Amna Waqar, Clinical Psychologist • MSc Psychology

Culturally sensitive care for young women navigating anxiety, relationships & growth.

I’m Hafiza Amna Waqar — a clinical psychologist based in Lahore, offering online and in-person sessions across Pakistan. My work is grounded in empathy, evidence-based practice, and deep respect for your story.

I primarily support young women, university students, and early-career professionals who are dealing with overthinking, academic pressure, family expectations, relationship confusion, self-doubt, and burnout — especially when it feels hard to talk openly at home.

Sessions are bilingual (Urdu & English), completely confidential, and paced at your comfort. No judgement, no rush — just a steady, safe space where you can say what you’ve been holding in.
